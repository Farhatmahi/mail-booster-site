The problem

Delivered isn't the same as seen.

Gmail sorts most brand emails into the Promotions tab. Your subscriber signed up, your email arrived, and Klaviyo counts it as delivered. Then it waits in a tab many people rarely open, under every other sale that week.

  • Klaviyo can't see the tab.

    Your dashboard reports delivery, not where the email landed. A healthy delivery rate can hide a big share of your list that never saw the email.

  • Every send costs the same.

    You pay Klaviyo for every profile you email. An email that sits unread in Promotions costs exactly as much as one that gets opened.

  • The usual fixes cost you sales.

    Plain-text emails, fewer images, asking subscribers to drag you to Primary. Some of it helps, but it means giving up the emails that sell.

Mail Booster fixes where your emails land without touching what you send.

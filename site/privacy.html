Privacy policy

Last updated: [date]

This policy explains what personal data Mail Booster collects, why, and what we do with it. It covers this website, booking a trial call, and the Mail Booster service itself.

Who we are

Mail Booster is a product of [InboxImplementor legal entity name], [registered address] ("we", "us"). You can reach us about anything in this policy at hello@mail-booster.io.

For data we collect about you as a visitor or customer, we are the controller. For the subscriber data inside your Klaviyo account, you are the controller and we act as your processor (see "Your Klaviyo account" below).

What we collect

When you visit this website

We measure visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It doesn't use cookies or local storage and doesn't build a profile of you. It records page views and basic, aggregated details such as the referring site, browser, device type and country. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels [confirm, and update this section if you add any].

Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, also processes technical data such as IP addresses and browser details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

When you book a call

Bookings are handled by [Cal.com / Calendly], embedded on our free trial page and on our partner page. When you book a trial call, the booking tool collects and shares with us:

  • your name and work email
  • your store URL
  • your monthly Shopify revenue range and how many campaigns you send per week
  • anything you add in the notes field
  • the time you chose, and the calendar invite details

When you apply to become a partner, it collects your name, work email and the details you give us about your agency and clients [match your partner booking questions].

We use this to prepare for the call, confirm your plan or partnership, and send you the invite. The booking tool also processes this data under its own privacy policy: [link to Cal.com or Calendly privacy policy].

The booking calendar is loaded from [Cal.com / Calendly] and may set its own cookies, and show its own cookie notice, when you use it on our site.

Your Klaviyo account

Mail Booster is a done-for-you service. To set it up and run it, we need access to your Klaviyo account. Specifically:

  • API keys. You give us a Klaviyo private API key [with these scopes: list them]. We store it [encrypted at rest, in: name the system], and only [who: e.g. the named team members who run your account] can use it.
  • Account access. We may also log in to your Klaviyo account [as a user you invite, with this role: confirm] to set up and adjust your campaigns.

Through this access we can see and change your campaigns, templates and sending settings, and we can see campaign results and the subscriber data stored in your account, such as email addresses, names and profile properties.

We use this access only to provide Mail Booster to you: to set it up, run it on your campaigns, run and read your A/B tests, and write your recaps. We don't email your subscribers on our own behalf, we don't copy your subscriber lists out of Klaviyo [confirm], we don't sell any of this data, and we don't use it for any other customer.

When you work with us

We keep the messages you send us by email or Slack, the recaps we send you, and the billing details needed to invoice you. Payments are processed by [payment provider]; we don't store full card numbers.

Why we use it (legal bases)

  • To provide the service you asked for (performance of a contract): booking data, Klaviyo access, recaps, billing.
  • To run and protect our business (legitimate interests): keeping records, preventing abuse, improving the service.
  • To meet legal obligations: tax and accounting records.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run Mail Booster, under contracts that limit their use of it:

  • Cloudflare (website hosting and cookieless web analytics)
  • [Cal.com / Calendly] (booking)
  • [Google Workspace] (email, calendar and Google Meet)
  • [Slack] (customer communication, if you use it with us)
  • [payment provider] (billing)
  • [where API keys are stored, e.g. password manager or cloud provider]

We never sell personal data. We may disclose data if the law requires it.
